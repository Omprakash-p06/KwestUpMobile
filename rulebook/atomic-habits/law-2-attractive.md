# The 2nd Law — Make It Attractive

> "Dopamine is released not only when you experience pleasure, but also when you anticipate it. Gamblers get a rush of dopamine right before they place a bet, not after they win." — James Clear

## Philosophical Principle

The more attractive an opportunity is, the more likely it is to become habit-forming. Habits are a dopamine-driven feedback loop. Desire and anticipation drive behavior.
Key strategies:
1. **Temptation Bundling:** Linking an action you *need* to do with an action you *want* to do.
2. **Social Norms and Belonging:** Joining a culture where your desired behavior is the normal behavior.
3. **Reframing Mindsets:** Shifting internal dialogue from "I *have* to do this" to "I *get* to do this."

## KwestUp Implementation

### 1. Temptation Bundling Support
KwestUp allows users and the AI Habit Compiler to configure temptation bundles:
- A habit can be paired with an attractive reward or sensory experience (e.g., "Only listen to favorite podcast while performing workout").
- In-app instructions display the bundled pairing alongside the minimum action.

### 2. High-Contrast Tactile Industrial UI
- KwestUp avoids dreary, bureaucratic forms. Completing an action triggers responsive tactile feedback: custom mechanical audio clicks, fluid animations, and crisp typography (`HankenGrotesk`, `JetBrainsMono`).
- Anticipating the tactile sensation of checking off an action stimulates positive dopamine anticipation without artificial manipulative dark patterns.

### 3. Identity Alignment Reminders
- When a reminder is dispatched, it highlights identity affirmation ("Take 2 minutes to confirm your identity as a writer") rather than punitive guilt ("You haven't written today!").
