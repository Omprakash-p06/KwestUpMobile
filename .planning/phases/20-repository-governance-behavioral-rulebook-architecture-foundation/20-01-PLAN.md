  <files>rulebook/ai/*.md, rulebook/rules/*.md</files>
  <read_first>4.0/KwestUp_4.0_Master_Plan.md, rulebook/README.md, .planning/phases/20-repository-governance-behavioral-rulebook-architecture-foundation/20-REVIEWS.md</read_first>
  <action>
    Author all 5 AI policy documents in `rulebook/ai/`. Each must include a "Privacy Boundary" section
    asserting that AI never receives or logs raw habit content, reminder times, cue text, or vault paths.

    1. `intent-parser.md`: Natural language -> structured intent parameters without hallucinated actions.
       - Input: raw user text string
       - Output: { action: CommandAction, params: Record<string, unknown> }
       - Boundary: AI may not write storage, call OS APIs, or schedule notifications directly.
    2. `habit-compiler.md`: Compiling goal statements into identity + habit + cue + 2-minute minimum action + normal target.
    3. `intervention-planner.md`: Surface arbitration (widget vs notification vs in-app) without nagware.
    4. `check-in-engine.md`: Reflection check-in protocols after missed cues or completion events.
       CRITICAL: Define check-in as a FINITE QUESTION BANK of exactly 5 structured questions — NOT
       open-ended LLM text generation. Questions are selected deterministically based on context.
       The 5 questions are:
       1. "What got in the way today?" (friction diagnosis)
       2. "How hard did it feel on a scale of 1-5?" (effort calibration)
       3. "Would an easier minimum action help?" (activation energy)
       4. "Should we adjust the reminder time?" (cue relevance)
       5. "Do you want to keep this habit?" (commitment check)
    5. `adaptation-engine.md`: Evidence-based adaptation: lowering activation energy when streaks fail twice.

    Author all 8 business rule documents in `rulebook/rules/`. Each must include:
    - `source` tag: "product-decision" or "atomic-habits-interpretation" with citation where applicable
    - `review_date` matching manifest.json
    - Counterexamples for edge cases

    1. `habit-creation.md`: [product-decision] Max 3 new habits simultaneously, mandatory 2-minute minimum action, required cue.
    2. `habit-modification.md`: [atomic-habits-interpretation, Clear ch.14] 80% consistency over 14 days before difficulty increase.
    3. `missed-habit.md`: [atomic-habits-interpretation, Clear ch.20] Immediate activation of "never miss twice" recovery mode; downscaling to minimum action.
    4. `rewards.md`: [product-decision] Real-world factual rewards (milestones, faster activation) over arbitrary gamified XP points.
    5. `reminders.md`: [product-decision] Quiet hours (default 22:00-08:00), daily cap (max 3/day), minimum gap (90 min).
       Include counterexamples:
       - "Quiet hours 22:00-08:00 crossing midnight: a 23:00 reminder shifts to 08:01 next day"
       - "DST transition: quiet hour times are wall-clock device time, not UTC"
       - "Multiple habits at 08:00: only the highest-priority habit fires; others are batched"
    6. `widgets.md`: [product-decision] Behavioral widget priorities: Today -> Next Action -> Don't Miss Twice recovery prompt.
    7. `overload.md`: [product-decision] Anti-burnout detection.
       CRITICAL: Must contain concrete thresholds:
       "Overload is declared when ALL of the following are true:
        - 4 or more habits are active simultaneously
        - The 7-day miss rate across ALL active habits exceeds 40%
        - The condition has persisted for at least 3 consecutive days
       When overload is declared, the app proposes pausing the lowest-streak habit."
    8. `privacy.md`: [product-decision] Absolute on-device guarantee:
       - Zero telemetry on habit content, habit titles, reminder times, or cue labels
       - Zero cloud transmission of any behavioral data
       - Logger output must NEVER contain raw habit content, cue text, vault paths, or notification bodies
         (these are considered user-identifiable behavioral data)
       - The logger.js SENSITIVE_KEYS regex must be extended in Phase 22 to cover 'habitTitle', 'cueText', 'body'
  </action>
