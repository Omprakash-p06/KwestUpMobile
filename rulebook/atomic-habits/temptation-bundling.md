# Temptation Bundling

> "Temptation bundling works by linking an action you want to do with an action you need to do. You're more likely to find a behavior attractive if you get to do one of your favorite things at the same time." — James Clear

## Philosophical Principle

Temptation bundling combines Premack's Principle ("more probable behaviors will reinforce less probable behaviors") with habit stacking.
The formula:
1. "After [CURRENT HABIT], I will [HABIT I NEED]." (Habit Stacking)
2. "After [HABIT I NEED], I will [HABIT I WANT]." (Temptation Bundling)

By tethering an immediate reward (a want) directly to an obligation (a need), the brain anticipates the dopamine payoff of the want, lowering the perceived resistance to beginning the need.

## KwestUp Implementation

### 1. Bundled Habit Schema
- KwestUp habits allow users to attach an optional `temptationBundle` note or media link to any habit definition.
- Examples supported in compiler templates:
  - "Only listen to favorite audiobook / podcast while exercising or doing household chores."
  - "Enjoy specialty coffee only while doing 30 minutes of deep writing."

### 2. Immediate Unlock Actions
- Upon marking a bundled habit complete, KwestUp displays a tailored satisfaction card:
  - "Obligation complete! Enjoy your 15 minutes of guilt-free browsing or podcast."
- This creates an unambiguous boundary where leisure is earned and enjoyable rather than accompanied by lingering procrastination guilt.
