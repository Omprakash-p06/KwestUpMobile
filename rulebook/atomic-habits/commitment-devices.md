# Commitment Devices

> "A commitment device is a choice you make in the present that controls your actions in the future. It is a way to lock in future behavior, bind you to good habits, and restrict you from bad ones." — James Clear

## Philosophical Principle

Humans suffer from hyperbolic discounting: we value immediate comfort far more than future utility. When we are thinking rationally in a calm state, we can use commitment devices to bind our future emotional, tired, or impulsive selves.
Examples:
- Purchasing individual food packages rather than bulk to limit portion sizes.
- Asking a hotel to remove the television from the room.
- Committing to a scheduled meeting with a personal trainer.

## KwestUp Implementation

### 1. Pre-Commitment in Habit Configuration
When setting up a habit or routine, KwestUp enables users to establish pre-commitments:
- **Locked Reminders:** Reminders scheduled in advance cannot be casually dismissed without a 3-tap confirmation or minimum action check-in.
- **Budget Envelopes:** In `BillingContext`, spending limits lock category thresholds before entering shopping environments.

### 2. Upfront Focus Sessions
- Starting a focus timer or deep work session creates a structured micro-contract. The timer prominently counts down, discouraging task switching or app hopping.
