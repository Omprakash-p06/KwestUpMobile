# Habit Modification & Progression Policy

## Rule Statement
A user cannot optimize or escalate the difficulty of a habit that has not first been standardized.
In KwestUp:
- **Difficulty Escalation Threshold:** A habit's `normalTarget` may only be upgraded if the user has achieved **$\ge 80\%$ consistency over the preceding 14 days** (at least 11 successful completions out of 14 scheduled days).
- **Maximum Increment Cap:** Target increases cannot exceed **10%** in a single adjustment (e.g., a 20-minute target cannot increase by more than 2 minutes in a single step).
- **Difficulty Downscaling:** Downscaling difficulty or falling back to the 2-minute `minimumAction` is permitted at **any time without restriction** to protect momentum.

## Rationale
"Standardize before you optimize." Users frequently feel ambitious after 2 or 3 successful days and double their target, precipitating exhaustion and abandonment. The 80% / 14-day rule ensures that automaticity has genuinely formed before additional load is introduced.

## Counterexamples and Edge Cases

> [!CAUTION]
> **Counterexample: Premature Escalation Attempt**
> A user begins "Study 15 minutes" on Day 1. On Day 4 (3-day streak), the user attempts to change the target to "Study 60 minutes".
> *System Response:* System advises: "You're building great momentum! To prevent burnout, Atomic Habits advises maintaining 80% consistency for 14 days before upgrading. You have 10 days remaining."

> [!TIP]
> **Permitted Downscaling Example:**
> A user is struggling with "Workout 45 minutes" after a 2-day illness. The user reduces target to "Workout 15 minutes".
> *System Response:* Immediately permitted and encouraged.
